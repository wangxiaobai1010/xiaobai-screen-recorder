# P2.4.1 Camera Motion Calibration

## Stage status

P2.4.1 establishes the user-selected real-time camera-motion feel baseline on
top of the P2.4 technical candidate. It does not change P2.4 from a technical
candidate into a final or formal freeze.

## Goal and non-goals

The goal was to make the final part of 1.6x and 2.0x zoom-in, return to Wide,
fixed-center travel, and comfort-zone Follow visibly decelerate and settle
naturally while preserving responsive interruption and reversal.

This stage did not change zoom ratios, F9/F10 semantics, fixed-target capture,
comfort-zone geometry, Cursor, Crop, OutputCanvas, Preview rendering,
RenderFrameTap, Encoder, MP4, recording control, safe-save behavior, or hotkey
activation. It introduced no automatic director, recorded timeline, future
cursor prediction, target clustering, UI, thread, timer, or new state machine.

## Starting behavior and rejected first calibration

The fixed camera originally used a shared 0.24-second SmoothStep timeline.
Although its endpoints were correct, its short fixed curve did not give the
last part of the movement a clearly perceptible deceleration. Follow already
used exact critical damping, but `omega = 36 rad/s` settled too quickly for the
deceleration to be easy to see.

The first calibration mixed 20% cubic ease-out into SmoothStep and relaxed
Follow stopping thresholds. User testing found it almost indistinguishable
from the original: the curve mixture was too small over a short timeline, and
stopping thresholds only control the final snap, not the visible approach
speed. That experiment was restored before the final implementation and no
cubic ease-out mixture remains in the final source.

## Cap read-only comparison and license boundary

A read-only comparison found that Cap's pronounced deceleration mainly comes
from velocity-bearing second-order motion in its post-recording rendering
path. The only principle carried into this real-time Preview implementation is
the general mathematics of velocity continuity and critically damped
second-order response.

No Cap AGPL source, structure, recorded-timeline implementation, future mouse
trajectory, automatic zoom rule, target cluster, or director behavior was
copied or translated. The implementation below was derived independently from
the analytic critical-damping equation and the project's existing camera
state model.

## Final motion model

For each scalar channel, with a target fixed for the current step:

```text
y = current - target
e = exp(-omega * dt)
temp = (velocity + omega * y) * dt
nextY = (y + temp) * e
nextVelocity = (velocity - omega * temp) * e
nextValue = target + nextY
```

The controller uses real QPC delta time and limits every integration substep
to 32 ms. Ordinary F9/F10/Wide retargeting changes only the target and retains
the current position and velocity. Initialization, Preview stop, camera
disable, exit/error fallback, exact settling, target crossing, and outward
boundary clamping explicitly clear the affected velocity.

The old SmoothStep helper remains as a general math utility and test subject,
but it no longer drives camera motion. The 0.48-second nominal settle value is
diagnostic ABI metadata only and is not used by integration.

## Final parameters

- Zoom: `omega = 14 rad/s`
- Fixed Center X/Y: `omega = 14 rad/s`
- Follow X/Y: `omega = 13 rad/s`
- Maximum integration substep: `32 ms`
- Zoom stop position epsilon: `1e-4`
- Zoom stop velocity epsilon: `1e-3`
- Center and Follow stop position epsilon: `2e-5`
- Center and Follow stop velocity epsilon: `5e-4`

Wide remains 1.0x, standard close-up remains 1.6x, and strong close-up remains
2.0x. Comfort-zone width and height ratios remain 0.42 and 0.36.

## Controlled A/B result

Candidate A was checkpointed at commit
`44131754314dad5c07277cb78b74b302a9766632` with Follow `omega = 16 rad/s`.
Candidate B changed only Follow X/Y to `omega = 13 rad/s`; all zoom, fixed
center, thresholds, step limits, retargeting, crossing, and boundary behavior
remained identical.

Xiaobai selected candidate B after direct Preview comparison. The reported
result was smoother Follow with a clearer, more natural deceleration before
the cursor-follow camera stopped, without noticeable sluggishness, drift,
jitter, or edge abnormality. Zoom-in and return-to-Wide remained consistent.
No further parameter tuning is planned for P2.4.1.

## Verification evidence

The final source review covers six camera-motion production files and one
camera-focused managed test file. Relative to the P2.4 baseline before this
report, production changes are 242 insertions and 101 deletions; focused test
changes are 253 insertions and 13 deletions. The production growth is the
shared analytic integrator, three fixed-camera velocity channels, real-delta
advancement, exact settling, crossing/boundary handling, and diagnostic
progress. The test growth supplies a camera-only entry point plus independent
checks for rest-to-target settling, 60/120 Hz equivalence, large-delta limits,
retarget/reversal continuity, preset sequences, Follow movement and stopping,
and boundary velocity clearing.

Release x64 builds and the following focused suite pass for candidate B:

```text
dotnet artifacts\bin\Release\x64\XbPreview.Managed.Tests.dll --camera-motion
XbPreview.Managed.Tests PASS: P2.4.1 camera motion
```

The focused evidence covers finite values, exact stopping, no overshoot,
velocity cleanup, frame-rate equivalence, maximum substep behavior,
mid-animation reversal, rapid preset switching, comfort-zone behavior, and
legal canvas boundaries. `git diff --check` also passes.

Recording, Encoder, MP4 output, safe-save, full historical gates, and 30/60
minute endurance runs were intentionally not executed because P2.4.1 changes
only real-time camera motion and those paths were outside its authorization.
The deferred 60-minute final integration gate still depends on a
RecordingController and safe internal stop channel.

## Handoff

P2.4 remains an encoding technical candidate seal, now with the P2.4.1 camera
motion feel baseline. The next product stage is P2.5A RecordingController; it
is not started by this stage.

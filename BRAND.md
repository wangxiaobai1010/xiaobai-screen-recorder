# Xiaobai Recorder Brand Guidelines

The Xiaobai Recorder source code is available under the MIT License. That license governs source-code rights; it does not make an unofficial fork or derivative an official Xiaobai Recorder release. These guidelines are separate from, and do not modify, the MIT License.

The official project brand identifiers are:

- 小白录
- Xiaobai Recorder
- the official Xiaobai Recorder logo
- the official Xiaobai Recorder application icon

Forks and derivative distributions are welcome. When distributing one to users, please use your own product name and branding. Do not use the official name, logo, or icon in a way likely to make users believe an unofficial distribution is official, endorsed, maintained, or published by the Xiaobai Recorder project.

Truthful descriptive references such as “Based on Xiaobai Recorder” or “Forked from Xiaobai Recorder” are welcome, provided the derivative is clearly identified as unofficial.

Other repository files and assets remain governed by any license or notice applicable to them. These guidelines address the official product identity only and do not claim rights in unrelated third-party assets.
